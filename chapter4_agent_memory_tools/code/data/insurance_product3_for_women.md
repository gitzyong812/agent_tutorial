## ## Key Benefits & Coverage Highlights

### 1. Pre- and Post-Natal Care

* **Routine Care:** Comprehensive coverage for routine OB/GYN consultations, ultrasounds, and mandatory prenatal screening scans.
* **Postnatal Recovery:** Includes follow-up checkups, pelvic floor rehabilitation, and professional lactation consultant support.

### 2. Delivery & Newborn Shield

* **Delivery Protection:** Covers expenses for Routine Vaginal Delivery, Medically Necessary C-Sections, and complications of pregnancy.
* **Newborn Care:** Immediate coverage for the newborn's nursery care, routine immunizations, and neonatal intensive care (NICU) within the first 30 days.

## ## Waiting Periods & Underwriting Rules

### 1. Maternity Care Waiting Period

* A **10-Month (300 Days) Waiting Period** applies to all pregnancy and childbirth-related claims.
> **Crucial Note:** Any maternity-related claims or conditions arising during this 10-month waiting period will be processed strictly according to the policy terms, and the company will not bear the liability for reimbursement. This is designed to prevent anti-selection.



### 2. Accidental Injury Responsibility

* Accidental injury protection has no waiting period and **is usually undertaken from the effective date of the contract**, subject to policy stipulations.

## ## Flexible Payment & Cancellation Policy

### 1. Premium Payment Maintenance

* Policyholders can select Annual or Monthly payments at inception. Any subsequent changes to the payment cycle must comply with the insurance company's administrative rules.

### 2. Cancellation Rights & Risk Warning

* **Within the Cooling-Off Period:** You enjoy a **15-day Free Look Period**. If you cancel the policy during this time, you will usually receive a **100% full refund** of the premiums paid.
* **After the Cooling-Off Period:** If you request termination after this period, the company will refund the **Cash Value** according to the contract.
> **Risk Warning:** Canceling the policy after the cooling-off period may result in **significant financial loss** as the cash value is typically much lower than the premiums paid. Meanwhile, you will lose your protection immediately.
